Delete your account and data

For the Android app com.tessera.blockpuzzle (“Tessera – Block Puzzle”).

Most players have no account to delete. Tessera is playable without signing in, and if you never signed in with Google, uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Delete your game history yourself, from the app

Open Your games from the history button, then choose Clear history. This deletes your past games from the device and from your account, immediately. No email needed.

Delete your whole account

To remove your account and everything attached to it, email sirajurrahman19@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Tessera account”.

Sending from the same address is what lets us match the request to your account — we have no other way to identify you, and we will not act on a request we cannot verify.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your leaderboard entry (display name and best score) Deleted
Your private profile (email address, Google profile name, which apps you have used) Deleted
Your game history Deleted
Your sign-in identity Deleted, so a later sign-in starts a fresh account
Anything stored only on your device Removed when you uninstall the app
Records of a purchase of the ad-free upgrade Held by Google Play, not by us. Deleting your Tessera account does not cancel or refund a purchase, and it does not remove Google's own billing records.

Requests are actioned within 30 days. Nothing is retained after that except where the law requires it.
