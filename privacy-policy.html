Privacy Policy — Tessera

Applies to the Android app com.tessera.blockpuzzle (“Tessera – Block Puzzle”).
Last updated: 31 August 2026

The short version. Tessera is playable without an account. If you never sign in, the game stores your progress only on your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising identifiers. Signing in with Google is optional and exists so your scores survive a reinstall. Nothing is ever sold.

What is collected, and why

DataWhenWhy
Advertising ID, device and app info, coarse location derived from IP Whenever ads are shown To serve and measure ads, and to limit how often you see the same one. Collected by Google AdMob, not by us.
An anonymous account identifier On first launch, if the network is available So a score can be attributed to “you” on the leaderboard without asking for any personal detail.
Chosen display name and best score When a score is published To show the global ranking. This is the only data that is publicly readable.
Email address and Google profile name Only if you choose to sign in with Google To recognise your account across devices and after a reinstall. Stored privately — see below.
Which of our apps you have signed into, and when each was last used Only if you sign in with Google So one account works across our apps, and to count how many people use them. Stored privately with your profile, never published.
Game history: score, stage, difficulty, duration and time of each run When a game ends To show you your own past games. Kept on your device; also backed up to your account if you are signed in.
Purchase status of the ad-free upgrade If you buy it To stop showing ads. Handled by Google Play Billing; we never see your payment details.

Your email address is never public

The leaderboard collection is world-readable — that is what makes a global ranking possible — and it contains only a display name, a score, and the time that score was set. Your email address, profile name and game history live in a separate area that security rules restrict to your account alone. No other player, and no anonymous visitor, can read them.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google’s handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms.

Before any personalised ad is requested, Tessera shows a consent prompt and respects the choice you make. Choosing not to consent does not remove ads; it limits them to non-personalised ones, which do not use your advertising ID to build a profile. You can reopen the prompt at any time from the app to change your mind, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android’s Settings under Privacy → Ads.

Buying the ad-free upgrade stops ad requests altogether.

Who else processes this data

  • Google AdMob — serving and measuring ads.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) — the account identifier, leaderboard entries, and your private profile and game history. This data is stored on Google servers in the United States (Firestore multi-region nam5). If you are in the UK or the EEA, that means your data is transferred outside your country; the transfer relies on Google Cloud’s standard contractual clauses.
  • Google Play Billing — the ad-free purchase.

There are no other third parties. Your data is not sold, and it is not shared for anyone else’s advertising.

Children

Tessera is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us using the address below and it will be deleted.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Game history — clear it from the app’s “Your games” screen. That deletes it from the device and from your account.
  • Leaderboard entry, email and profile — follow the steps on the account deletion page. Requests are actioned within 30 days.
  • Everything local — uninstalling the app removes all data held on the device.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, and to object to how it is processed. Use the contact address below to exercise any of them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of the page will change and the updated version will be published here before the change takes effect.
